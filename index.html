About us

M/S Sai Air Compressor

M/S Sai Air Compressor, established in 2012 and run by Jitendra Kumar Samal from Kandasara, Jajpur, is an ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certified contractor. We are an Odisha PWD Class A contractor and have worked with steel, cement, aluminium and chemical plants and government departments.

We handle all types of Hilti work, core and diamond cutting, concrete breaking, grouting, compressor work, sand blasting, waterproofing and mechanical fabrication and erection. Every job starts with marking the line, the depth and the rebar, so openings land where the drawing says and the rest of the structure stays untouched.

FAQ

Before you call

Short answers to what clients usually ask first.

How do I get a quote?

Send the type of work, the material (slab, wall, beam or rock), the approximate size or number of holes and your site location. Photos help. We reply with a price after a call or a site check.

Do you take government work?

Yes. We are an Odisha PWD Class A contractor for government building and road works, and we also hold GST and Udyam registrations. You can view all three in the certificates section.

Can you cut openings in an existing building?

Yes. Diamond cutting and core cutting are made for this: door and window openings, shafts, and holes for pipes, cables and AC lines, with the area marked and protected first.

Will cutting damage the rest of the structure?

Cutting is done to marked lines with low vibration, and breaking is kept controlled and limited to what has to come out. Tell us if the structure is loaded or has a design drawing, and we plan around it.

What is the difference between chemical bolts and anchor bolts?

Chemical bolts are set in resin and suit heavy loads, rebar dowels and tight edge distances. Anchor bolts grip mechanically and suit machines, racks and fittings. We can advise which one fits your load.

Do you work outside Jajpur?

Yes. Our past and current work includes Kalinganagar, Barbil, Keonjhar, Damanjodi (Koraput) and Jamshedpur, along with Jajpur. Send your site location and we will confirm.
